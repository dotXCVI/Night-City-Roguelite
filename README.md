# Night City Roguelite

**Eighteen fights. Three acts. One way home.**

A roguelite mode for Cyberpunk 2077 2.5 + Phantom Liberty. Start a fresh V,
skip the story, and fight your way through Night City arena by arena - picking
your next fight, your rewards and your build as you go. Flatline and you wake
up back in your apartment with nothing but the Cred you banked. Spend it, gear
up, go again.

Built entirely from the game's own stuff: real weapons, real cyberware, real
perks, real gangs. No custom models.

## What you get

- **Pick your fight.** After every arena, choose the next door by what it
  pays - chrome, your gun, quickhacks, perks, a patron's backing, or
  something stranger. HOT doors hit harder and pay double.
- **Patrons.** Arasaka, Militech, the Voodoo Boys, Maelstrom, the Aldecaldos
  and the Afterlife - four of them work the city each run. Their effects
  change how fights play, and they're built to chain: MARK a target, SHOCK
  the crowd, set it on fire, BLAST what's left. Fifteen duos, seven
  legendaries, a favour for going all in.
- **Builds that come together.** Twelve fighting styles - blades, monowire,
  dual pistols, smart guns, netrunning and more. Lean into one and it ranks
  up, with a signature move at rank III.
- **One gun, built up.** Your main weapon takes every upgrade: tune it up,
  load it with weapon effects, trade it without losing them, and evolve it
  into an iconic.
- **Trauma Team has your back.** Full health every fight and one cover a
  run - flatline once, get back up.
- **Overdrive.** Beat the Act 1 boss, pick an operating system, and earn an
  ultimate: ZERO HOUR stops time, RAMPAGE turns you into a wrecking ball,
  BLACKWALL fries every enemy in the room.
- **Gigs, bounties and side deals.** Take a gig from Wakako or Rogue, hunt a
  cyberpsycho for NCPD, gamble with a bookie, let Misty read your cards.
- **Your apartment is the hub.** Pick your kit in the armory, start runs from
  your computer, save between runs. The front door stays shut.
- **Meta progression.** Put bosses down and the fixers owe you favors - call
  them in for new kits. Cred buys the terminal's upgrades: ten rows, two ways
  to play each.
- **Assist mode** if you just want the power fantasy.

## Get it

You need Cyberpunk 2077 **2.5** with **Phantom Liberty**, plus these (all
free on Nexus Mods - grab the latest):

- [RED4ext](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/2380)
- [redscript](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/1511)
- [Codeware](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/7780)
- [TweakXL](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/4197)
- [ArchiveXL](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/4198)

Then download the
[latest build](https://github.com/dotXCVI/night-city-roguelite/releases/latest/download/NightCityRoguelite.zip)
and either drop the ZIP into Vortex / MO2, or copy its `r6` and `archive`
folders into your game folder.

Your story saves are never touched.

**Updating?** Delete `r6/scripts/NightCityRoguelite` and
`r6/tweaks/NightCityRoguelite` from your game folder first, then install the new build the same way. Your Cred and unlocks carry
over. Full steps are in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md#updating-from-an-older-version).

## First run

1. Main menu -> **ROGUELITE**. Make your V.
2. The screen stays black for up to 45 seconds while the mod sets you up.
   Don't touch anything. You'll wake up at home.
3. Read the guide that pops up. Then head to the **armory** - the weapon room
   in the back - and open the stash to pick your kit.
4. Sit down at your **computer** and hit **START RUN**.

Closed a screen by accident mid-run? **F7** brings back whatever's waiting on
you - your reward, the Fixer, the next door.

## Known issues

- Saving is locked during a run, and quitting mid-run counts as a flatline.
- If the armory has no stash on your character, press F7 inside the room
  instead.
- English only for now.
- A future game patch can break the opening skip. If it does, the mod waits
  out the opening call instead.
